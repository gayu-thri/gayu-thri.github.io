Strategy-Selection Steering in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning

Steered DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B away from low-confidence reasoning strategies by probing hidden states at paragraph boundaries in middle-late layers and applying mean-difference steering vectors. Boosted accuracy from ~50% to ~58% on AIME (probe AUC ~0.87). CS 639, Spring 2026.

LLM Fine-Tuning for Constraint-Following at 1B Scale

Fine-tuned Llama 3.2 1B with LoRA (r=8, 0.007% of params) on RECAST-30K for multi-constraint instruction-following. LoRA lifted Hard CSR from ~28.9% to ~80.0% (5-fold CV, <1% fold variance), substantially outperforming full fine-tuning (~33.2%, which suffered catastrophic forgetting). Built a 9-stage preprocessing pipeline with SHA-256 deduplication and MinHash LSH near-deduplication (Jaccard 0.85), expanding 30K → 90K examples via back-translation and lexical augmentation. STAT 453, Spring 2026.

PyTorch LoRA HuggingFace RECAST-30K MinHash LSH LLM Fine-Tuning
